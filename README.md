Hi, I'm Veronica! 👋
I'm a mathematical epidemiologist, and I build models and tools that help health systems act early, from malaria to maternal mental health. I'm a Research Assistant at the Swiss Tropical and Public Health Institute, where I develop MalReBay, a Bayesian R package that tells malaria treatment failures apart from new infections. I also lead engineering for MESO+, a platform that helps mothers notice when they're struggling and connects them to care.

I studied statistics, then spent four years at Dalberg Research building machine learning models and data pipelines for survey research. I went on to an MSc in mathematical epidemiology at AIMS Rwanda as a Mastercard Foundation Scholar, where my thesis became MalReBay. I work mostly in R, Stan and Python, and I like turning complex data into tools that people on the ground can actually use.

Outside work, I'm usually watching anime or playing sport: badminton, lawn tennis, ping pong and the occasional fencing bout.
